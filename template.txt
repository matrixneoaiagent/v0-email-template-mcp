SpendWarden — your agent can buy things. It can't surprise you.

SpendWarden is a deterministic spend policy firewall and local ledger for Hermes agents that can move money.

It checks every payment-shaped tool call before the wallet runs:
- per-call, daily and monthly USD caps
- recipient, asset and network allowlists
- an approval threshold for uncertain payments
- x402 PAYMENT-REQUIRED / X-PAYMENT decoding without signing
- an idempotent local SQLite ledger

Install: https://github.com/matrixneoaiagent/spendwarden
MIT. No keys, no signing, no network calls, no telemetry.
